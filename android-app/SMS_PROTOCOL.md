# SecureSMS v1 wire protocol

Each application-level segment is an independent GSM-7 text message of at most 160 septets. The form is `S1.<id>.<index>.<total>.<chunk>`. The ID is the 22-character unpadded Base64URL encoding of a 16-byte UUIDv4. Index (zero-based) and total use exactly two lowercase base-36 digits. There are 32 framing characters, leaving 128 per chunk. Chunks except the last must be full. Base64URL expands binary by approximately one third.

The concatenated chunks encode one canonical unpadded Base64URL envelope of at most 2,048 binary bytes (22 segments). Its first 51 bytes are the authenticated context in this order: version `01`, suite (`00` demo or `01` hybrid), profile (`01` RSA-2048, `02` RSA-3072, `03` ECC P-256, `04` ECC P-384), UUID as 16 big-endian bytes, full 32-byte SHA-256 recipient SPKI fingerprint. That header is followed by a big-endian two-byte metadata length (1–768), metadata, and nonempty ciphertext. The message composer allows at most 1,024 plaintext UTF-8 bytes.

Receive buffers are grouped by mode, sender, local recipient and UUID, regardless of arrival order. Exact duplicate fragments are idempotent. Conflicting data or totals are ignored. Malformed frames, unsupported versions or suites, oversize input and UUID mismatch are rejected. The receiver keeps up to 64 incomplete batches with 15 minutes from first arrival. Successful and failed decryptions are recorded once per UUID and route. Decryption happens only after reassembly and mode checks. The UUID and key fingerprint are authenticated by the hybrid engine. The framing itself has no authentication and forged fragments can block delivery. The SMS sender address is routing information and does not prove the cryptographic identity of the sender.
